House Points after each lesson

A lesson ends when nobody in that class has done any work for 15 minutes (at least 3 students must have worked, so home learning isn't counted). While this page is open it checks every 2 minutes and emails you the top 5 for each lesson that has ended. Lessons that ended while it was closed (in the last 24 hours) are emailed when you next open it.

Who can get a House Point: only students who worked actively for at least 30 minutes in that lesson (typing, running and checking work: being signed in doesn't count), and only one House Point per student per lesson. Totals restart every Monday.

How the top 5 is chosen: new tasks completed in that lesson, weighted by difficulty. Python: Beginner ×1, Intermediate ×1.5, Advanced ×3 per exercise. Design and IT: practice passed ×1, criterion submitted ×2, strand completed ×3. Ties go to the student with more tasks.

3 · Email alerts form

The lesson emails are sent through a Google Form that emails you each response, so your Gmail stays hidden.

Not made yet? It takes about 3 minutes:
  1. In Google Forms, create a new form called SDC Alerts.
  2. Add five Paragraph questions named type, student, email, teacher, data, in that order.
  3. Responses → three dots → turn on Get email notifications for new responses (signed in with your personal Gmail).
  4. Three dots at the top → Get pre-filled link → type x in every question → Get link → copy it and paste it below.

4 · Email verification

New students and teachers must press a link sent to their @sisd.ae email before they can get in. Personal emails can't be used. Accounts that already existed keep working. The emails are sent from your Gmail by a small script you set up once:

  1. Go to sheets.new to create a Google Sheet, and name it SDC Verification.
  2. Click Extensions → Apps Script. Delete what's there and paste the script below (use Copy script), then click 💾 Save.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment → the ⚙️ next to "Select type" → Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy.
  4. Click Authorize access, choose your Gmail, then Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow (it's your own script).
  5. Copy the Web app URL and send it to Claude to switch verification on.
  6. Test first: sign up with your own @sisd.ae email and check the email arrives (look in Junk too).

Saving also passes the two sheet links, locked, to every approved teacher, so their dashboards work on any computer. Teachers never see the links themselves.
