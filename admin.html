House Points after each lesson

A lesson ends when nobody in that class has done any work for 15 minutes (at least 3 students must have worked, so home learning isn't counted). While this page is open it checks every 2 minutes and emails you the top 5 for each lesson that has ended. Lessons that ended while it was closed (in the last 24 hours) are emailed when you next open it.

How the top 5 is chosen: new tasks completed in that lesson, weighted by difficulty. Python: Beginner ×1, Intermediate ×1.5, Advanced ×3 per exercise. Design and IT: practice passed ×1, criterion submitted ×2, strand completed ×3. Ties go to the student with more tasks.

2 · Email alerts form

The lesson emails are sent through a Google Form that emails you each response, so your Gmail stays hidden.

Not made yet? It takes about 3 minutes:
  1. In Google Forms, create a new form called SDC Alerts.
  2. Add five Paragraph questions named type, student, email, teacher, data, in that order.
  3. Responses → three dots → turn on Get email notifications for new responses (signed in with your personal Gmail).
  4. Three dots at the top → Get pre-filled link → type x in every question → Get link → copy it and paste it below.

These links are saved on this computer only. To use the admin page on another computer, paste them there too.
