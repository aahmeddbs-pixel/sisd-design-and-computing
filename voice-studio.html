Tips for a great recording
  • Use a quiet room. A headset or earphones with a microphone sounds best.
  • Speak like you're talking to one student: warm, calm and a little slower than normal.
  • Computer Science videos: the video plays silently while you record. Read each line when it lights up; it stops by itself at the end.
  • Strand explanations (Design, Grades 6–8): write your own script for each strand (a starter script is filled in for you to change), press Record, read it paragraph by paragraph and press Stop. Students hear it on that strand's task page.
  • Design and IT videos: read the paragraphs in order at a steady pace, with a short pause between them. Press Next line (or the space bar) to move the highlight, then Stop when you finish.
  • Need a break? Press ❚❚ Pause, then ▶ Continue when you are ready: the video waits for you, so your voice stays in step.
  • Made a mistake? Press Record again. Only the last take is kept.
